Additional notes:


1. FlashOnline have 1024k memory (or 512x16) organization, to write files 256k (28 / 29F200)

MMSB must be 0x0C, for 512k (29F400) it is 0x08, for 1024k (29F800) it is 0x0, respectively.
2. CS – is Check Sum. Are just 8-bit additive of the rest of the request, no carry.
